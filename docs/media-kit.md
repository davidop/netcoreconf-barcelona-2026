## Social copy in Spanish

**Título:** El aprendiz de agente: magia, telemetría y gobernanza bajo control 🪄

**Descripción:** ¿Y si un agente pudiera optimizar costes y rendimiento sin saltarse las políticas? Érika Cepeda y David Oliva Paredes exploran un supervisor multiagente con .NET, Microsoft Agent Framework, Quality Gates y Mission Control. Tres escenarios: optimización, bloqueo por gobernanza y rollback. La demo pública utiliza datos sintéticos y una arquitectura reproducible.

**Links:** [GitHub](https://github.com/davidop/netcoreconf-barcelona-2026) · [Interactive slides](https://davidop.github.io/netcoreconf-barcelona-2026/)

## Notes on imagery

- The repository illustrations are **original SVG artwork**; they are not photographs or authentic Playwright captures.
- The wizard is a stylized conference character, **not a Microsoft Copilot or Foundry official mascot**.
- The actual screenshots are test artifacts in [browser E2E Actions](https://github.com/davidop/netcoreconf-barcelona-2026/actions/workflows/e2e.yml).
- Icons copied from third-party projects retain all relevant trademark rights: see [attribution](../assets/brand/README.md).
- For the GitHub social-preview setting, export the SVG to a raster format at its native 1280 × 640 dimensions; the connector cannot update repository metadata automatically here.
